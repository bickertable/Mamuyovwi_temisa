Work with me

Sequencing advice from someone who has actually shipped in these markets.

I work with founders and product leaders building in emerging markets, in fintech, payments, and social commerce, who are past the idea stage but stuck on sequencing.

What to build first. How to earn regulatory trust before scaling. How to structure distribution when formal infrastructure does not exist yet.

I do not do generic startup coaching. My clients have traction and are making it up as they go on strategy. They need someone who has built products in these markets, not studied them from outside.

What a sprint looks like

Four weeks to a defensible build order.

01Map the constraintWeek 1
02Test the sequence against itWeek 2
