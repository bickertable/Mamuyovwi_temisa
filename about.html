About

Ten years building products in the markets everyone else studies from a distance.

Mamuyovwi Temisa is a Lead Product Manager, entrepreneur, and technology strategist with over ten years of experience building digital products across fintech, payments, and social commerce in Africa.

He holds a Bachelor of Engineering in Production and Manufacturing Engineering from the University of Benin and an Executive MBA from Tekedia Institute, where he sharpened his thinking on business strategy, growth, and innovation.

His entrepreneurial journey began in 2014 while he was still studying at the University of Benin, where he founded his first startup, Yomabox. In 2018, he launched Alphally, a technology platform that connected people to events across Nigeria through an online ticketing system.

Alongside building startups, he developed his expertise through product and growth leadership roles at Baxi and Onafriq, gaining deep experience in fintech, digital payments, and product innovation across East and West Africa.

His career has been driven by a simple belief: that African entrepreneurs can build world-class technology companies by solving local problems with bold ideas, disciplined execution, and relentless curiosity.

Africa's next generation of enduring companies will be built by founders who solve the harder, more local problem first, rather than importing playbooks designed for different markets.

Through his writing, he shares the lessons, patterns, and hard-won principles behind the continent's boldest fintech successes, for entrepreneurs, product leaders, and anyone building something meant to last.
