The book

From the Ground Up

How Africa's boldest entrepreneurs built their empires. Thirteen companies, traced through the decisions that actually mattered.

This is not a collection of success stories dressed up in the language of inspiration. It is an account of what it takes to build durable companies where the infrastructure, the capital, and the regulatory precedent do not yet exist.

Every company in the book built something specific because it operated in Africa, not in spite of it. Interswitch built the first African fintech unicorn because Nigeria's banking system was fragmented and cash-dependent, not despite it. JUMO built a credit infrastructure company because most of its customers had no formal credit history, not despite it. The constraint was the opportunity in every case.

Who it is for

Founders, product leaders, and anyone building something meant to last.

If you are building in an emerging market, the book is a set of patterns you can apply directly. If you are building elsewhere, it is a study in what product decisions look like when there is no safety net underneath them.

The closing chapter distils everything into seven principles. That chapter is the framework I use with advisory clients, and it is published in full on this site.
